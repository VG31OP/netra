# Privacy Policy & Architecture

NETRA is designed with a strict privacy-first model:

1. **Zero Telemetry**: No tracking identifiers, error reporting telemetry, or background analytics.
2. **Offline-First**: All network operations are directed exclusively to user-specified local or target addresses.
3. **No Mandatory Accounts**: No authentication or cloud accounts are required.
4. **Data Isolation**: All logs, scan records, and captures remain on the local filesystem.
